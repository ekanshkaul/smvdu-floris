Ecological Sampling Methodology

Field documentation at Shri Mata Vaishno Devi University adheres to standardized quadrat sampling along elevation gradients spanning 780m to 880m MSL in the sub-Himalayan Shivalik zone. Biomass and carbon stock estimations utilize non-destructive allometric equations adapted from the Forest Survey of India (FSI) guidelines for Pinus roxburghii and subtropical mixed dry-deciduous formations.
